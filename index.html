About the Book

A practical guide created to encourage Nigerian students to transform ideas into meaningful action.

Turning Campus Ideas into Reality

This book is designed for students who have ideas, ambitions and dreams but may not know where to begin.

It explores practical campus business opportunities, the student mindset, consistency, discipline, time management, branding and strategies for growing beyond campus.

The goal is simple: help students start small, think practically and build valuable experience while pursuing their education.

About the Author

Comrade Suleiman Naim Gambo

Director Of Special Duties NANS JCC Nasarawa State

Comrade Suleiman Naim Gambo is a student leader and advocate for entrepreneurship, innovation and practical opportunities for Nigerian students.

Through his experiences within the student community, he developed a strong interest in helping students recognize the value of their ideas and take practical steps toward building them.

Turning Campus Ideas into Reality was written as a practical resource for students who want to explore entrepreneurship while pursuing their education.
