About the Book

Many Nigerian students have ideas but lack the confidence, clarity, or starting point to turn those ideas into something practical.


Turning Campus Ideas into Reality is a practical guide created to help students develop an entrepreneurial mindset, identify realistic campus opportunities, start small, remain consistent, and balance academics with business.


The book focuses on practical opportunities that can be started with little or no capital and encourages students to use their time, creativity, skills, and networks to create value.

About the Author

Comrd. Suleiman Naim Gambo is a final-year Economics student at Nasarawa State University, Keffi, an entrepreneur, student leader, and youth development advocate.

He currently serves as Director of Special Duties, NANS JCC Nasarawa State Axis, and has held various leadership roles focused on entrepreneurship, skills acquisition, empowerment, and student development.

He is also the founder of NSG Global Enterprises and is passionate about helping young people turn their ideas into practical opportunities.

Turning Campus Ideas into Reality was inspired by his belief that students can build meaningful ventures while pursuing their education.
